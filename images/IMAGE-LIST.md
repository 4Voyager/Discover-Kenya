# Image list

Drop your photos into this folder using **exactly** these names. The site loads every picture from here,
so a missing file shows an empty box. 58 images in total: 14 places × 4, plus 2 site-wide.

## Rules
- Format: **.jpg** for every file.
- Hero pictures must be big or they look soft: the six places used in the Home hero (**maasai-mara, mount-kenya,
  diani-beach, amboseli, lamu, lake-nakuru**) and `explore-hero-bg.jpg` should be **at least 2400 px wide**
  (2560 or 3200 is better), saved at JPEG quality 80–85. A 1600 px photo stretched across a 1920 px or retina screen is the usual cause of blur.
- Every other image: **1600–2000 px wide**, 16:9 works best. Keep the subject near the centre: cards and the Home hero rail crop to portrait.
- File size: aim for 250–600 KB each. Run big photos through Squoosh or TinyPNG.
- `{slug}.jpg` is the main image. It is used for cards, the Home hero, the place's own hero and the first gallery picture.
- `-2`, `-3`, `-4` fill the rest of that place's gallery. Make them different views, not copies.
- Old `-bg` and `-fg` files are no longer used. You can delete them.
